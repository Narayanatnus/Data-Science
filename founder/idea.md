# The Learning Experience franchise, Naperville

- What it is: a franchised The Learning Experience (TLE) child care and early-education center in Naperville, Illinois.
- Who it is for: working parents with young children aged 1 to 6.
- What it sells, at what price: full-time child care and early education; planned tuition $1,300 to $1,400 per child per month.
- Where and how: a physical center in Naperville, IL (exact site not yet chosen); enrollment through the TLE brand and local marketing; care delivered on site.
- Budget and constraints: $250,000 of the founder's own money to invest, plus up to $400,000 borrowed ($650,000 total available). Not yet stated: the site (new build or conversion), whether talks with the TLE franchise team have started, whether the founder will run the center or hire a director, and licensed capacity.
