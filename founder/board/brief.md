# Board brief: The Learning Experience franchise, Naperville

## The idea (from the founder)
- What it is: a franchised The Learning Experience (TLE) child care and early-education center in Naperville, Illinois. TLE is an existing national child care franchise brand; the founder would be a franchisee.
- Who it is for: working parents with young children aged 1 to 6.
- What it sells, at what price: full-time child care and early education; planned tuition $1,300 to $1,400 per child per month.
- Where and how: a physical center in Naperville, IL. Exact site not yet chosen. Enrollment through the TLE brand plus local marketing; care delivered on site.
- Budget: $250,000 of the founder's own money, plus up to $400,000 borrowed ($650,000 total).

## Not yet known (do not assume values for these)
- Site: location within Naperville, new build or conversion, lease terms.
- Status with the franchisor: whether talks or a franchise disclosure document review have started; the franchise fee, royalties and total build-out cost have not been provided.
- Operator role: whether the founder runs the center day to day or hires a director; the founder's experience in child care or management was not stated.
- Licensed capacity (number of children), staffing plan, and age-group mix.
- Local competitors and local demand: not yet researched (`/founder-competitors`, `/founder-consumer`).
- Costs, margins and break-even: not yet modelled (`/founder-cfo`).
